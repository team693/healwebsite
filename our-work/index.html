Our approach

یہ صفحہ فی الحال انگریزی میں ہے۔ اردو ترجمہ جلد شامل کیا جائے گا۔

Our work is grounded in values that go beyond service delivery. We focus on sustainability, designing programs that build long term self reliance through skills, education, and community ownership. From low cost educational models to health interventions that empower families, not just treat them, we ensure every initiative leaves a lasting impact.

Our approach is deeply grassroots, rooted in listening to real needs and co-creating solutions with the communities we serve. Whether in a classroom, clinic, or relief drive, we begin where change is most needed: on the ground, with the people.

What truly sets us apart is our commitment to good intentions and ethical impact. We reject performative charity and lead with sincerity, humility, and purpose. Our fundraising, awareness campaigns, and on ground efforts are guided by dignity driven storytelling and responsible messaging that uplifts rather than exploits. We believe in serving without spectacle, empowering without judgment, and helping without creating dependency.

Health

Access to healthcare is a fundamental right, not a privilege.

Refer a patient

Our Health Department provides dignified, need based medical support to underserved individuals and families across Karachi and beyond.

Since inception, our health efforts have been grassroots driven and deeply personalized. We work closely with public hospitals and verified cases to sponsor life saving treatments, cover surgical expenses, and provide diagnostic support to those who fall through the cracks of the formal healthcare system.

What we have done so far

  • Sponsored 500+ medical cases at government hospitals, ranging from emergency treatment to paediatric surgeries and orthopaedic interventions.
  • Organised blood donation and free medical camps offering checkups, screenings, and health counselling for low income neighbourhoods.
  • Promoted hygiene awareness among schoolchildren and women through interactive sessions and kits.
  • Donated essential equipment and supplies to under resourced healthcare facilities to improve service delivery.
  • Identified gaps in public care through hospital visits and field research, informing future interventions.
  • Provided BiPAPs to 3 government hospitals during the Covid era.

Our Health Team, especially Umar Jusani (now Muqaddas Foundation), has been heavily invested in providing comprehensive help to patients in need.

Education

Education is the heartbeat of transformation at Heal.

Read the ElevenLabs story

Through our flagship initiative, Project Rihla, we provide underprivileged youth, especially girls, school dropouts, and adult women, with access to meaningful, skills based, and values driven learning. Our vision is not just to teach but to equip individuals to think critically, earn independently, and live ethically.

We believe education must be relevant to real life. That is why we combine language development, digital literacy, vocational training, and ethical education into a single holistic curriculum. Students not only improve their academics, but also gain confidence, problem solving skills, and a mindset of self reliance.

  • Learning centres serving lower secondary students in Shah Faisal Colony and Korangi.
  • Hybrid digital classes using Google Classroom, hands on projects, and interactive STEM based learning.
  • Foundational early education for young children with a focus on ethical and cognitive development.
  • Career counselling and language coaching to help students prepare for the real world.
  • Vocational skills like computing, arts, woodworking, and digital marketing for income generation.
  • Role plays, field trips, and community projects that turn classrooms into real life learning labs.

Awareness

Heal’s Awareness Department sheds light on often overlooked social issues: mental health, personal ethics, financial literacy, and environmental responsibility.

Through sessions at universities, public institutions, and community spaces, we engage youth in meaningful conversations that promote self awareness, empathy, and critical thinking. With formats like skits, seminars, and interactive talks, we spark dialogue that leads to personal and societal change.

Love for Humanity

At the core of Heal lies a deep commitment to compassion in action.

Our Love for Humanity efforts span from visiting old homes and orphanages to providing crisis relief during natural disasters and economic hardship. Through initiatives like White Card food support, Sasta Rashan programs, plantation drives, and community celebrations, we aim to restore dignity, hope, and connection. Every act, no matter how small, reflects our belief that kindness can heal, uplift, and unite.
