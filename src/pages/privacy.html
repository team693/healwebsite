Privacy

How this website handles your information.

What this website collects

This website has no accounts and does not use advertising or tracking cookies. It remembers your language choice (English or Urdu) in your own browser only.

Forms

Our volunteer, partner and internship applications use Google Forms. What you enter there is sent to Heal Social Foundation through Google and handled under Google’s privacy policy. We use it only to respond to your application.

Fonts

Text on this website uses Google Fonts, so your browser requests font files from Google’s servers.

Donations

Bank transfers go directly to our Meezan Bank account. Gifts through GoFundMe are handled under GoFundMe’s own terms and privacy policy.

The people we serve

We do not publish identifying details of patients or students without their consent, or their guardian’s consent.
