Our organisationہمارا ادارہ

یہ صفحہ فی الحال انگریزی میں ہے۔ اردو ترجمہ جلد شامل کیا جائے گا۔

Heal Social Foundation is a Karachi based nonprofit organization, registered under Section 42 of the Companies Act, 2017 and the Federal Board of Revenue (FBR) Pakistan. Formally founded in July 2024, Heal is committed to creating a sustainable, ethically conscious, and empowered society.

Our mission revolves around grassroots empowerment through innovative education, inclusive healthcare, community awareness, and humanitarian support, namely Health, Education, Awareness and Love for Humanity: Heal.

Our flagship initiative, Project Rihla, provides educational opportunities to underprivileged children, particularly girls, school dropouts, and adult women. By blending linguistic, digital, ethical, and critical thinking skills, we aim to nurture responsible and self reliant citizens for tomorrow.

“What is an ocean but a multitude of drops.”
Each effort we make contributes to a larger movement, bringing lasting change to communities that need it the most.

Our teamہماری ٹیم

At the heart of Heal Social Foundation is a team of passionate and diverse professionals, ranging from educators and engineers to doctors, psychologists, and chartered accountants. This multidisciplinary leadership enables us to tackle challenges holistically across education, health, and social development.

Our faculty members and managers are not only highly qualified but deeply empathetic, committed to nurturing potential in every student. They use interactive, tech enabled teaching methods and culturally rooted mentorship to build confidence, independence, and practical skills in our learners.
