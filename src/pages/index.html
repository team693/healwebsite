Why we exist

Too many patients reach hospital when it is already late

In Karachi’s low income neighbourhoods, illnesses that were preventable or simple to treat early often arrive at government hospitals when treatment is hard, costly and less likely to work. Heal works at both ends of that journey.

In the community

Reach people before illness becomes an emergency

  • {{icon:check}}Free medical camps with checkups, screenings and health counselling
  • {{icon:check}}Hygiene sessions and kits for schoolchildren and women
  • {{icon:check}}Learning centres in Shah Faisal Colony and Korangi
{{icon:hand-heart}}

At the hospital

Make sure treatment is not stopped by cost

  • {{icon:check}}Life saving treatment and surgery costs for verified cases
  • {{icon:check}}Diagnostic support for patients the formal system misses
  • {{icon:check}}Equipment and supplies for under resourced facilities

Questions

Before you give

Anything else? Email team@healsocialfoundation.com

Is Heal a registered organisation?
Yes. Heal Social Foundation is a not for profit company licensed under Section 42 of the Companies Act 2017 (SECP CUIN 0265422, licence No. 2057) and registered with the Federal Board of Revenue (NTN E445846). Our accounts are audited by Zain Suhail & Co., Chartered Accountants.
Where does Heal work?
In Shah Faisal Colony, Korangi and Nath Khan Goth in Karachi and the surrounding settlements, and with government hospitals that serve these communities.
How are patients chosen for support?
We work closely with public hospitals and support verified cases: life saving treatment, surgery costs and diagnostic support for people the formal health system misses.
Can I give Zakat?
Write Zakat in your bank transfer reference, or email us after you give, so your gift is recorded and used as Zakat.
Can I give from outside Pakistan?
Yes. Our GoFundMe page accepts cards from anywhere. You can also send an international transfer to our IBAN; email us if your bank needs more details.
Can I give my time instead of money?
Yes. Doctors, teachers, students and professionals volunteer with us. Apply to join
Can I refer a patient who needs help?
Yes. Email or call us with the patient’s situation and the hospital they are at. Contact details
