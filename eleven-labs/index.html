ElevenLabs Impact Programme

Breaking barriers in education for underserved and differently abled students

How Heal and ElevenLabs are making learning accessible in Pakistan, one audio lesson at a time.

About Project Rihla

Heal Social Foundation operates Project Rihla, an after school and weekend learning program serving 50 students in the under resourced areas of Shah Faisal Colony and Korangi in Karachi, Pakistan.

Our mission focuses on providing foundational education, vocational training, and digital literacy to students who face various learning challenges, with a special emphasis on creating inclusive learning environments.

The partnership

By converting written materials into multilingual audio, we have made learning more accessible and engaging for all our students, particularly those with learning disabilities, language barriers, and visual impairments.

  • Lesson materials turned into bilingual audio in English and Urdu
  • Voice based exercises for language practice
  • An offline audio library for students without reading support at home
  • Vocational training made more accessible through natural, warm narration

Spotlight

Hafsa’s story

Hafsa, a determined young student and the first visually impaired participant in our Rihla Vocational Training Program, shows what accessible technology can do. Traditional written materials and worksheets were completely inaccessible to her, creating significant barriers to her education.

The solution

We converted written lectures, worksheets, and educational materials into engaging audio using ElevenLabs’ natural voice technology, creating:

  • Bilingual audio lessons in English and Urdu with familiar, warm narration
  • Interactive audio worksheets that guide students through exercises step by step
  • Accessible vocational materials for computer skills and other practical subjects

The impact

What once seemed like insurmountable barriers became gateways to learning. Hafsa became so connected to the experience that she said she wanted to “talk to the voice”, and she looks forward to each new audio class.

This shows how technology can create truly inclusive education, where students with visual impairments do not just participate, but thrive alongside their peers.

Listen to one of Hafsa’s audio assignments

Looking forward

Hafsa’s success is just the beginning. With ElevenLabs’ support, we are building an audio library that will serve current and future students with diverse learning needs, so every student can access quality education regardless of ability.

“With courage in her heart and technology by her side, Hafsa is paving the way for a more inclusive future.”
